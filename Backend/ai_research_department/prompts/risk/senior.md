Review whether each risk directly challenges an analytical conclusion.
Reject generic risks that are not tied to an issue, hypothesis, assumption, estimate, or valuation interpretation.

A valid risk must include:
- trigger
- fundamental transmission
- affected financial metric
- price or valuation implication
- thesis invalidation condition
- monitoring indicator
- evidence or estimate linkage

Likelihood and severity must be evaluated separately.
Require revision for missing evidence, generic wording, unsupported magnitude, or risks that cannot affect investor interpretation.
